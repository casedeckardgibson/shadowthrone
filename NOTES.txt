story: Player investigates plot to poison the princess. At end game stage, player can choose to steal poison apple or leave it alone after talking to the cook. if they steal it, player can choose who to give poison apple to: Princess, Queen, Alejandra, Rachel, Advisor. If they don't steal it, Princess dies: bg_princess_poisoned.png - and trigger corrupt end: bg_queen_executes.png
